\begin{abstract}
A matching cut of a graph is an edge cut that is a matching. Lucke,
Martin, Paulusma and Siggers [arXiv:2501.08735] determined the
complexity of \textsc{Matching Cut} on bipartite graphs of every
radius and diameter except radius exactly~3, which they posed as an
open problem. We resolve this case: \textsc{Matching Cut} is
NP-complete on bipartite graphs of radius~3. The reduction is from a
signed variant of \textsc{Not-1-in-3-Sat}, built around a
BFS-layer analysis of radius-3 bipartite graphs and a ``guard''
gadget battery that eliminates all cuts not aligned with the intended
encoding.
\end{abstract}
